## Saturation fix (Phase 4, `CENTURY_V2_SOFT`)

Legacy clip dynamics rail the bounded variables against 1.0; the soft (logistic) update keeps them off the bounds. Fraction of survivor-years a variable spends within 0.01 of a [0,1] bound, N=50000:

| Variable | Legacy pinned % | V2 (soft) pinned % |
|---|---:|---:|
| W | 8.9 | 0.0 |
| Rd | 27.6 | 0.0 |
| G | 32.1 | 0.1 |
| Tr | 23.0 | 5.0 |
| H | 40.9 | 0.2 |

Survivor concentration W (median across still-ongoing worlds) by decade:

| Path | 2080 | 2090 | 2100 | 2110 | 2120 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Legacy W | 0.60 | 0.50 | 0.41 | 0.32 | 0.24 |
| V2 (soft) W | 0.70 | 0.70 | 0.70 | 0.69 | 0.68 |

**Finding: does bimodality survive?** Under soft dynamics the survivor wellbeing median drops from a railed 1.00 (legacy) to 0.75, and concentration W no longer deconcentrates late-century: legacy W falls 0.60 -> 0.24 across 2080-2120 while the soft path holds it roughly flat (0.70 -> 0.68). The late-century deconcentration is therefore a clip artefact and does not survive. The outcome distribution stays dominated by the disempowerment / irreversible-bad mass with a smaller good tail, so it remains broadly bimodal, but the middle is no longer negligible: the middling share (turbulent_transition + muddling_degraded) rises from 2.26% (legacy) to 5.43% under SOFT alone (11.09% under the full V2 set). Net: bimodality survives in weakened form; the deconcentration narrative does not.
